Solution 1. The function f (x, y) = |xy| satisfies: f (x, y) = f (−x, y) = f (x, −y) = f (−x, −y). Therefore, while
searching for the critical points in the interior points, we can assume x ≥ 0 and y ≥ 0. The only critical point of xy is
(0, 0). Note that it lies on the boundary of the ball. To this we must add also the points where f is non-diﬀerentiable,
these are all the points satisfying xy = 0. At all such points f vanishes.
When looking for the critical points of f on the boundary of the ball, we again use f (x, y) = f (−x, y) = f (x, −y) =
f (−x, −y). Therefore it is enough to check the critical points of xy on the circle (x − 1)2 + (y + 1)2 = 2, and also to check
the points where the function might have the diﬀerentiability problem, i.e. |xy| = 0. The critical points of xy on the circle
(x − 1)2 + (y + 1)2 = 2 are obtained in the standard way. (e.g. by the condition grad(xy) ∼ grad((x − 1)2 + (y + 1)2 − 2).)
